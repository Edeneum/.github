# Edeneum

**Wisdom for internet-native citizens.**

Eden is where learning began. Adam's first work in the garden was to name the animals; Solomon spoke of everything from the cedar to the hyssop, the birds, the ant and the stars. The mind is a garden, and it has to be tended.

Edeneum is the house of study for the builders of a second Renaissance: the theoretical academy of [Renaissance](https://renaissanceml.com). Where Renaissance makes the object, Edeneum examines the idea. Every shelf of the house is read here, closely, cited exactly and carried into the life of the builder: the classics, the Tanakh and Psalms, the Mishnah and Talmud, Leonardo's notebooks, the classical physicians, the archives of law, history and diplomacy. The library is filed by colour, one pigment to every discipline, so you always know where you are.

**The Codex** is the library: each work in a Basic Edition, the text and its translation, and an Enhanced Edition rebuilt for study.

**Read With Me** reads every shelf aloud, one passage at a time.

**The Garden** is the fellowship that reads together.
